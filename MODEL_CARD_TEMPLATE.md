## Purpose

MQDeck AI is an independent messaging-operations assistant designed to help with
administration, troubleshooting, configuration, and analysis in environments that may
include IBM MQ. It is not an IBM product and is not endorsed, sponsored, supported, or
certified by IBM.

## Training data

The public build accepts only original, local project content with explicit commercial and
redistribution permission. It does not ingest vendor documentation, websites, customer
data, support cases, proprietary software, or remotely collected content. See
`sources.json` for document-level provenance and license decisions.

## Limitations and safety

The model may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsafe. It must not be treated as vendor
documentation or authoritative operational guidance. Human review is required before any
state-changing action. Destructive commands require impact analysis, evidence, backup or
recovery planning, and explicit operator confirmation.
